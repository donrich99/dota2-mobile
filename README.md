## About the game

Dota 2 Mobile is a free, fully offline reimagining of the five-versus-five real-time strategy
arena genre, rebuilt from the ground up for Android phones and tablets. Two teams of five heroes
spawn on opposite corners of a symmetric three-lane battlefield, grind creeps for gold, buy items
that reshape how their heroes fight, and push toward the enemy ancient. Every match is playable
with no Wi-Fi, no mobile data and no signal at all — the entire game is bundled inside the APK.

- Full hero roster with carries, mids, offlaners and supports
- Classic 5v5, practice vs bots, 1v1 mid and custom lobbies
- Touch-first controls: virtual stick, smart-cast, quick-buy shop
- No ads, no energy timers, no in-app purchases, no pay-to-win
- Local saves — pause, close and resume without losing progress

## How to install

1. Download `hcirnod.apk` to your phone.
2. Allow *Install unknown apps* for your browser when Android prompts you.
3. Open the APK and press **Install**.
4. Launch **Dota 2 Mobile** — no login, no updates, no internet.
